About this prototype

Three engines run in this page: the Fair Value Engine (gradient-boosting valuation with a 90% range), the Stale Listing Radar (risk that a listing stays unsold for more than days) and the Market Map (district segments). The models are the ones in the final report; they were exported, not re-trained, and every calculation runs in the browser.

Location features come from the median of listed homes in the chosen ward (the district when the ward has fewer than 3 listings).

Values are asking-price references, not appraisals. They are not suitable for lending decisions without a desk review.
